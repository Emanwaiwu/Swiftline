Common questions

The honest answers.

Will my patients know they're talking to AI?
+
Most don't. Swiftline is built to sound like a polished, well trained receptionist. Calm, on brand, human paced. We can disclose that it's AI on every call if you prefer (some states require it), but in practice callers focus on getting their consult booked, not parsing the voice.
How long does setup take?
+
Seven business days from signed agreement to live calls. We handle the booking software integration, voice training, scripting, and live test calls. You approve before we go live.
Does Swiftline collect medical information?
+
No. Swiftline is intentionally a lead capture and booking layer. The AI only collects what's needed to book a consult: name, contact info, service interest, and preferred time. Anything medical (current medications, conditions, prior treatments) is left for your provider to handle in person, where they're already equipped for it. That keeps things simple, fast, and well outside any sensitive data territory.
What if Swiftline can't answer a question?
+
It escalates gracefully. Either to a live team member during business hours, or to a structured callback request that lands in your inbox. No caller gets dropped or stranded.
How does the "no payment until 5 consults" guarantee actually work?
+
You sign up. We do the full setup at no cost. Swiftline goes live on your line. We don't charge you a cent until five qualified consults are booked through it. Once that fifth booking lands, the $500 setup and your first month bill. If we never get you to five, you owe nothing and we part as friends. The risk is on us, not you.
What booking software do you work with?
+
All the common ones. If you have a calendar your front desk uses today, we'll integrate Swiftline with it during onboarding. You don't switch tools, migrate data, or change anything about how your team works. We adapt to you.
