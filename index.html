Common questions

Will customers know they're talking to AI?
+
Most don't. Swiftline sounds like a calm, professional dispatcher. By the time they realize, the job is already booked. We can disclose that it's AI at the start of every call if you prefer (some states require it), but in practice callers care about one thing: getting a tech out to their house. They don't care who takes the call as long as somebody does.
How does Swiftline handle real emergencies?
+
It triages. Gas smell, no heat in winter, no AC in a heat wave, water on the floor. The AI knows what's actually urgent versus what can be scheduled for tomorrow. Tier-1 emergencies get dispatched to your on-call tech immediately or escalated to a human if you've configured that. Routine stuff gets booked into the next available slot. You set the rules during onboarding.
How long does setup take?
+
Seven business days from signed agreement to live calls. We handle the field service software integration, voice training, dispatch logic, emergency triage rules, and live test calls. You approve before we flip the switch.
What if Swiftline can't answer a question?
+
It escalates cleanly. Anything technical, complex, or sensitive routes to your on-call tech or dispatcher during business hours, or gets logged as a structured callback that lands in your inbox. No caller gets dropped or stranded.
