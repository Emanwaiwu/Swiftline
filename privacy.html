Privacy Policy

Last updated April 2026

This is a brief, plain-English summary of how Swiftline handles your information.

What we collect

When you fill out the contact form on our site, we collect your name, email, and the name of your company. If you book a demo, our calendar provider (Cal.com) collects your name, email, and the time you choose. That's it for the marketing site.

How we use it

We use your contact info to reach out about Swiftline. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than talking to you.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-first tool that doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites, and doesn't collect personal information. We see aggregate traffic numbers, not individual visitors.

For Swiftline customers

If your HVAC company is using Swiftline as a service, we collect call data and lead information on your behalf. That data is yours, we never use it for any purpose other than running your service, and we never share it with third parties. Specific terms of how we handle that data are covered in the service agreement you sign during onboarding.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete any information we have about you at any time. Email eman@getswiftline.com and we'll handle it within 30 days.

Questions

Email eman@getswiftline.com and a real human will get back to you.
